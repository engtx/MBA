# Q1 Decision Reflection (draft)

Copy/paste into the Canvas prompt. Decisions: Forge Bikes, Mountain, Rio de Janeiro.

---

**What specific marketing factors did you consider in this decision round? Briefly describe.**

I treated Q1 as an STP call: one segment, one city I can reach with a single outlet, and a name that positions us. On the customer side I looked at what each segment values, not who is biggest. Mountain’s highest needs (durability, terrain, low weight, custom fit) match what carbon fiber actually does, so the company fit is there. I passed on Recreation even though it is larger. Meaningful targeting means excluding people, and Rec is more price sensitive and wants easy/simple more than performance. For place I used Mountain’s share of each city, not total demand, and picked Rio. I am hoping New York and Amsterdam pull the volume and Speed teams. Forge Bikes is the positioning: breaking ground, hardened and durable, custom-built.

**What information provided in the simulation was key to the choices you made and how did you use it?**

The customer-needs table was the main input. I compared the standout need in each segment: Recreation is easy to ride, Mountain is can handle rough terrain, Speed is light weight / speed. Carbon fiber is already light, and the custom 3D fit helps the ride, but rough terrain is the need that actually distinguishes Mountain and that I can design around. I also used price willing to pay. Mountain sits in the middle (~$1,120). That told me I can build a capable bike without racing Rec on price or needing Speed’s $1,300 ceiling. The 12-month demand by city is what sold me on Rio. It is not the biggest Mountain pool (NYC is), but it has the highest Mountain share of local demand, so one store is less wasted on people I am not targeting.

**Are you confident in your decision? Why or why not?**

Overall I feel confident given the data we had. I am not targeting the largest or the richest segment on purpose. The risk is that if other students read the same research the same way, Rio / Mountain gets crowded and the size disadvantage shows. Q1 is also supposed to stay tentative until the test market comes back, so I expect to adjust.

**What other information would you have liked in making this decision and why?**

I would have liked a finer cut inside Mountain. The survey treats them as one segment, but trail riders are not all the same: extreme technical, casual slopes, long-distance endurance. Who they are and how they ride would change the bike I build in Q2. I also would have wanted operating costs by city and any signal of where competitors are opening. We had to infer crowding from market size.
